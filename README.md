## 💡 Part A: Strategic Business Case Highlights
*Target Document:* [`Week11_Business_Case_CarsonWambua.pdf`](Week11_Business_Case_CarsonWambua.pdf)

- **Operational Problem:** 1,800+ monthly NOC incidents, 45-minute average MTTR, and severe alert noise causing recurring SLA penalties costing **$320,000 annually**.
- **Proposed Solution (OpsPulse AI):** Real-time operational intelligence combining unsupervised machine learning (`IsolationForest`) anomaly detection with grounded SRE runbook automation to cut MTTR to 12 minutes (**-73%**).
- **Financial Return (Show the Math):**
  - **Initial Investment (CAPEX):** $45,000
  - **Annual Operating Costs (OPEX):** $12,000 / year
  - **Total Year 1 Cost:** $57,000
  - **Gross Annual Savings:** **$320,000 / year** ($208K SLA penalties avoided + $49.5K engineering hours saved + $62.5K churn mitigation)
  - **Net Year 1 Benefit:** $\$320,000 - \$57,000 = \mathbf{\$263,000}$
  - **Year 1 Net ROI:**
    $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\$263,000}{\$57,000} \times 100\% = \mathbf{461.4\%}$$
  - **Payback Period:**
    $$\text{Payback Period} = \frac{\$45,000}{(\$320,000 - \$12,000) / 12} = \mathbf{1.75 \text{ Months (53 Calendar Days)}}$$
- **Top 2 Risks & Mitigations:**
  1. *Model Drift & False Positive Alert Fatigue:* Mitigated via dynamic Isolation Forest contamination threshold slider and dual-mode heuristic fallback.
  2. *NOC Workflow Adoption Friction:* Mitigated via a 14-day silent shadow deployment model and 1-click CLI runbook generation.
- **Board Recommendation:** Unanimous **"GO"** approval request for $45,000 CAPEX to initiate 6-week phased deployment.
