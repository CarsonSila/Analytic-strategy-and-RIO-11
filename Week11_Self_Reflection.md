# 📝 Mock Interview Self-Reflection
**Author:** Carson Sila Wambua  
**Assignment:** Week 11 Strategic Business Case & Mock Interview  
**Target Role:** Senior Operational Data Analyst / Operations Intelligence Lead  
**Date:** September 2026  

Reflecting on my 30-minute operational data analytics mock interview, several key strengths stood out. Structuring my responses using the STAR methodology allowed me to communicate high-pressure operational crises with precision and composure. Drawing directly from my Network Operations Center (NOC) experience at Quavatel Limited and my work building OpsPulse AI, I anchored my answers in quantifiable metrics—specifically highlighting our 73% MTTR reduction (45 to 12 minutes), 94% anomaly precision using Isolation Forest, and $320,000 in annualized SLA penalty avoidance. The panel noted that framing technical machine learning choices around tangible business ROI and executive payback periods (53 days) demonstrated strong executive presence and cross-functional empathy.

However, the session illuminated two critical areas for improvement. First, under deep technical questioning regarding mathematical hyperparameter tuning, I initially leaned into high-level operational descriptions rather than immediately discussing mathematical loss formulations and contamination ratios. Moving forward, I will lead with exact algorithmic mechanics before shifting to business impact. Second, I noticed a slight tendency to accelerate my speech pacing during complex multi-part crisis scenarios. Practicing deliberate conversational pauses and checking in with the interview panel will enhance executive clarity and ensure more collaborative dialogue during high-stakes evaluations.
